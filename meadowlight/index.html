Weather we work with

We read the sky before we pick up a spade.

Cumulus humilis

Plant out. Small fair-weather clouds mean a calm, mild day. Young roots settle best now.

Stratus

Transplant shade lovers. Soft grey light keeps leaves from scorching while they recover.

Cumulonimbus

Wait a day. Heavy rain washes seed out of fresh beds. We stake tall stems and go home.
